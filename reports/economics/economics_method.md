# Economics method

Evidence boundary: **SOURCE_REPORTED** for the cited tariff; **ASSUMED** for slide estimates and scenario inputs; resulting project economics are **NOT_VERIFIED** until matched M0 runs, site billing, costs and currency conversion are established. No site savings are claimed.

The [EVN publication of Decision 2892/QD-BCT](https://evn.com.vn/userfile/User/tcdl/files/2026/11/1_QD-BCT_2026_2892-20261104131123509.pdf) sets production rates effective 1 November 2026, excluding VAT. The example config selects low-voltage supply (up to 1 kV): off-peak 1,274, normal 1,966, peak 3,586 VND/kWh. Actual rates depend on the supply voltage. [EVN's Decision 963/QD-BCT summary](https://www.evn.com.vn/d/vi-VN/news/Quyet-dinh-so-963QD-BCT-ve-khung-gio-cao-diem-thap-diem-va-gio-binh-thuong-cua-he-thong-dien-quoc-gia--60-2025-507803) gives off-peak 00:00–06:00 daily, peak 17:30–22:30 Monday–Saturday, normal the remaining hours, and no Sunday peak. The cited production schedule lists energy rates, not a separate demand charge; applicability of any contractual demand charge is NOT_VERIFIED. Default zero is an assumption, not a claim about every contract.

For each aligned step, cost = dt_h × (import kW × band price − export kW × export price), with export price either explicit or alpha_ex × import price. Alpha is an assumed contractual credit, not an EVN export entitlement. Timestamps are local billing time; numeric hours assume Monday–Saturday. Steps must align with TOU boundaries and cannot straddle them. If configured, add monthly peak import kW × demand rate for each represented calendar month; use full representative billing months, since partial months cannot establish a billable peak. Annualisation scales the whole run cost by annual operating hours/run hours, so demand results also require representative complete months and an appropriate operating-hours multiplier; do not extrapolate a short run's peak into a bill.

For matched time grids, annual net saving = annualised (baseline electricity cost − new electricity cost) − incremental annual OPEX + explicitly supplied annual cooling saving. Cooling is never inferred from electricity. Simple payback = incremental CAPEX / annual net saving; report **not reached** when saving <= 0. NPV = −CAPEX + sum of annual net saving/(1 + discount rate)^year over the horizon. IRR solves NPV = 0 when a meaningful root is found, otherwise None. These are constant annual cash flows without taxes, inflation, replacement or residual value.

FIN-A is software on existing regen-to-AC; FIN-B optimises an existing shared DC installation; FIN-C is a full retrofit. Slide-16 hardware/server amounts are ASSUMED USD estimates, not supplier quotes. OPEX, installation and requalification amounts are unknown, not demonstrated zero; populate them before using results. Sensitivity uses ASSUMED slide-16 flat prices $0.046/$0.078/$0.132 per kWh, export alpha 0/0.5/1 and CAPEX multipliers 0.5/1/2. These USD scenarios are distinct from the sourced VND tariff: use one consistent currency and document any exchange rate. Compare matched instances, profiles, bus limits and tardiness; report paired uncertainty from actual runs separately.
