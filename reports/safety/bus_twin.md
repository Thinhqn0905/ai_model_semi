# DC-bus plan screening

Boundary: **SIM_ASSUMED_PROFILE** throughout. These are simulated controls, not hardware measurements or evidence of hardware safety. The model screens simulation logic only; independent protection and hardware validation remain necessary.

Station-by-slot source and sink powers are nonnegative DC-side kW. Each station approaches its next slot target at the assumed ramp limit. With energy E = C V²/2, the model integrates dE/dt = P_src - P_sink + P_afe - P_chop - P_loss, with power converted to watts. Positive AFE power injects into the bus: its reference is clip(K (V_nom - V), ±P_afe_max), followed by a first-order lag. Chopper power rises linearly from zero at V_chop to its maximum at V_max. Loss is an assumed fraction of min(P_src, P_sink), default zero; it is not the AC energy ledger. I_link is conservatively approximated by max(total source, total sink)/V; direct power is min(total source, total sink). Voltage, current and direct-power violations reject a plan.

Defaults (all **ASSUMED**, task specification and legacy example): nominal 400 V; limits 380–420 V; 0.04 F; 20 kW AFE, 2 kW/V droop, 0.08 s lag; chopper threshold 415 V and 15 kW maximum; 80 A current limit; 32 kW direct limit; 20 kW/s per station ramp; 1 ms step; zero direct loss. The ramp, direct limit and zero loss are modeling choices, not calibrated device limits. DS02 passive approximately 1 s traces do not identify the 80 ms AFE response, subsecond dynamics or equipment ramps (see reports/data/ds02_report.md).

The simulated domain is the union of slot-boundary windows ±2 s, clipped to the plan. State is carried from one window to the next without integrating omitted gaps; the initial state is idle at nominal voltage. Consequently slow relaxation, long ramps and events in omitted gaps are not assessed, and chopper energy covers simulated windows only. The zero-energy clamp and near-zero-voltage current approximation are numerical safeguards, not a valid physical model of collapse.

Controls and exact outputs: [controls.json](../../runs/twin_v1/controls.json), boundary **SIM_ASSUMED_PROFILE**. Synchronous four-station source burst: rejected for overcurrent and overvoltage. Smooth balanced ramp: approved. Demand exceeding AFE capacity: rejected for undervoltage. Equal source and sink: voltage remains at nominal with zero chopper energy. The pytest gate output is [pytest.txt](../../runs/twin_v1/pytest.txt).
