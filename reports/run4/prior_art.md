## Actual incremental contributions

- A reproducible, scoped integration of production-constrained rolling scheduling, regenerative energy ledger, modeled electrical limits and dynamic screening for a battery-cycling reference cell. This is a project contribution, not a claim of globally first use. SIM_DATA_PROFILE / SIM_ASSUMED_PROFILE, depending on the run; see [decisions](../../docs/decisions.md).
- An explicit decomposition and matched-baseline framework separating existing regeneration, local reuse, shared topology, conventional and rolling scheduling, and forecasts. The residual-source eligibility condition and negative converter-burn-in control constrain where a shared link could help. Analytic/model evidence; site eligibility NOT_VERIFIED.
- Facility-plus-cell tariff counterfactuals and an evaluation of optimization versus forecasting are scoped research results; public facility traces are unsynchronized with cell traces. SIM_DATA_PROFILE; site bills and realized benefit NOT_VERIFIED. The project decisions retain optimization for scheduling and ETA as advisory, without attributing energy or peak benefit to AI.

## Unsupported novelty claims

- Inventing regenerative loads, channel-to-channel energy reuse, shared DC buses, factory energy management, energy-aware scheduling, peak/demand-charge scheduling, or battery state/runtime prediction.
- Being first to combine these elements globally, or asserting that vendors lack energy-aware scheduling: the review is not exhaustive and vendor policies are NOT_VERIFIED.
- Claiming the modeled shared-link advantage is software-only, or that vendor-reported performance is a ReGenATE result.
- Claiming learned AI improves energy, peak or production decisions over a matched non-AI baseline, or that ETA accuracy proves downstream savings.
- Claiming measured plant savings, validated compatibility, protection certification, semiconductor-site applicability or commercial payback from simulation; these remain NOT_VERIFIED.
